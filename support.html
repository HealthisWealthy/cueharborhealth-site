Common questions

Is KindCue a medical service?

No. KindCue is a personal health-journaling and organization tool. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a healthcare professional.

Where is my journal information stored?

The current beta is designed to store core journal information locally on the device. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Can I share information with a healthcare professional?

KindCue is being designed to help users organize and export information they choose to share. Users should review any exported information before sending it to another person or service.

How do I report a problem?

Once KindCue enters public testing, the official support email and app-store support channel will be listed on this page.
