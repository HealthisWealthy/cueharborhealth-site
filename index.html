  • Simple daily logging
  • Body-map symptom tracking

Our first product

KindCue

KindCue is being built as a calm, user-friendly companion for personal health journaling. It is intended to help people keep better notes and prepare clearer timelines for conversations with healthcare professionals.

Capture the day

Record symptoms, mood, meals, medications, sleep, and other events without needing a complicated spreadsheet or notebook.

See the timeline

Review entries over time to make it easier to remember when something started, improved, or changed.

Share more clearly

Use organized summaries as a reference during appointments. KindCue does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice.

Designed with care

Privacy and clarity are part of the product.

KindCue is being developed with a privacy-first approach. The current beta uses on-device storage for core journal information and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in its current dependency set.

Some optional device features, such as speech recognition, notifications, image selection, printing, or sharing, may involve operating-system or third-party platform services selected by the user.
